How to read this book

  • ●○○ Easy · only open chords you already know.
  • ●●○ Medium · slightly faster changes or a trickier key.
  • ●●● Stretch · longer songs, key changes, stamina builders.
  • Keys are chosen so every song works with open chords only — no barres.
  • Too low or high for your voice? Slap a capo on and the chords stay the same, the pitch moves.

Chords here are simplified campfire versions for practice — not note-for-note transcriptions. Build the groove with one song, then steal its pattern for the next four: that's the whole trick. Made with HTML and CSS only — no JavaScript, no libraries.
